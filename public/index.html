About

Julia is a brand identity and fashion designer, but above all, she loves surfing.

That's why she created Dame una Ola, a brand born out of her endless obsession with catching waves.

Even though she considers herself a pretty mediocre surfer, her entire life revolves around chasing the best waves and spending as much time in the ocean as possible.

Dame una Ola is a reflection of that love for the ocean, made to follow you wherever your search for the perfect barrel takes you.
